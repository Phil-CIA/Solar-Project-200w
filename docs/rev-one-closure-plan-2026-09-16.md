## 1. Checkpoint Reality Check

Good progress is real: Rev 1 schematic and netlist are synchronized with 0 ERC errors, 163/172 BOM lines have LCSC matches, and the Battery-First architecture (DEC-025 through DEC-029) is fully documented.

But **Gate 1 (Component and Stress Review) in the implementation plan has not actually exited.** Gate 2/3 work (schematic edits, netlist/ERC regeneration) proceeded on several components that are still `OPEN` or `HOLD` in the derating review. Treat that gap as the top risk right now: it is possible to reach "PCB looks done" before the power path is proven to survive 125 W continuous.

**2026-09-17 (DEC-049/DEC-050) update - real panel Voc is higher than previously assumed.** Phil supplied the actual panel spec sheet (Renogy `RSP100DCT`, confirmed via its Amazon listing): single-panel Voc=28.5V (not the 22.79V previously used from an unattributed Amazon-listing citation), giving a corrected 2S string Voc=57.0V at STC (not 45.58V). Using the panel's own disclosed -0.29%/degC temperature coefficient (DEC-050) to replace an earlier generic cold-weather guess: **U6 VIN/BIAS (59V absolute max) is exceeded starting at just -10degC (2S Voc ~62.8V)** - a mild, everyday-plausible cold morning, not an extreme - making this the single highest-priority open item in this whole document, ahead of any Gate 4 work. **D2 (SMBJ60CA TVS, 64V standoff) is razor-thin at -10degC (~1.9% margin) and exceeded by -25degC.** A third item, **C7 (2.2uF/50V ceramic)**, has its own voltage rating outright exceeded by the corrected STC Voc alone. See DEC-049/DEC-050 in `docs/decisions-log.md` for the full recompute; see the Track A table below for updated dispositions.

**2026-09-17 (DEC-051) update - resolved at the root cause by rewiring the array 2P instead of 2S.** The LM51772's own RECOMMENDED VIN operating max (48-55V) is below the corrected 57.0V STC 2S string Voc, not just its 59V abs max under cold conditions - meaning even STC operation was outside spec, not only a cold-weather edge case. Phil selected rewiring the PV array 2P (parallel) instead of 2S (series): `PV_IN_POS` now sees single-panel Voc (28.5V STC, ~33.9V at -40degC) instead of the string Voc, resolving U6 VIN/BIAS and D2 standoff with no new parts. **This reopens every PV-side current-based margin computed under 2S assumptions** (F1 fuse sizing, input-bank C19/C21/C30/C31 ripple current, Q4/D1 ID margin) for re-verification at the new current level (Isc ~8.90A vs the old 4.45A). C7 still needs its planned 100V swap regardless of wiring. See DEC-051 in `docs/decisions-log.md` and Section 1a's replacement note in `hardware/mppt/envelope-decision-worksheet.md`.

**2026-09-17 (DEC-052) update - the reopened current-based margins are re-verified.** Used Excel COM automation to actually recompute the LM51772 Quickstart Tool workbook at the 2P operating point (VIN 20/23.6/34V) instead of assuming every current-based figure simply doubles: the **input-bank ripple-current demand is actually LOWER** (7.75A vs the old 8.56A) because it is driven by the converter's own operating point, not directly by panel Isc - a correction to DEC-051's own cautious assumption. Q4/D1 current margin re-derived by simple ratio against the new Isc_parallel=8.90A: ~59.5% margin, PASS. F1 fuse amperage re-sized to a provisional 15A (up from 10A); the underlying voltage-rated fuse family/part selection (DEC-035) remains the one still-open item in this whole chain. See DEC-052 in `docs/decisions-log.md`.

**2026-09-17 (DEC-053, then DEC-054) update - F1 fuse-family question resolved by an operational decision, not a hardware swap.** DEC-053 initially selected a MIDI/AMI 58V-class bolt-down fuse family for F1. Phil then decided (DEC-054) to keep F1 on the existing Keystone `3522-2` 32V-class clip instead - the residual cold-Voc exceedance (~1.9V/~5.9% over 32V, only below approximately -25 degC) will be mitigated with an active, firmware-controlled parasitic load on the panel (display/fan/status LEDs, a few mA) rather than a fuse/footprint change. This closes DEC-035 as an accepted-risk-with-mitigation, not a component change, but adds a **new, not-yet-implemented firmware requirement** (Track F) that must exist before real cold-weather PV exposure. See DEC-054 in `docs/decisions-log.md`.

This document exists to make that gap explicit and trackable. Update dispositions in place (`OPEN` / `HOLD` / `REWORK` / `PASS`) as work closes; do not delete completed rows, mark them `PASS` with a date.
