# LM51772 (U6) 12.8 V bring-up findings, 2026-11-06

## Result

U6 produces 12.8 V from 8-30 V in with PCM disabled. The earlier ~5 V ceiling is tied to the CSA/CSB peak-current sense path: with the normal signal the IOUT status trips; shorting or attenuating the sense signal allows regulation. The physical cause of the excessive sensed signal is not yet established (switching transient/noise versus real peak current).

Below about 14 V in, Vout only follows Vin because of the PCM window (D9 PCM_WINDOW_LOW reset 0xC = 30 %, about 9.0-12.8 V for a 12.8 V target: converter stops switching and passes Vin through; below the window it regulates to the lower edge, 0.7 x 12.8 = 8.96 V). This is not a sense problem. With `U6 PCM OFF` (D9 = 0x20) the converter held 12.7-12.8 V at 8/10/12 V in; see `rev1-u6-pcm-off-retest-2026-11-06.md`.

## STATUS_BYTE (0x78) evidence

- Sense connected: 0x58 = OFF + IOUT + INPUT, Vout stalls at about 4.6 V.
- CSA/CSB shorted: 0x08 (INPUT only) for the whole 10 s window, IOUT and OFF clear.
- INPUT (bit 3) re-sets right after W1C at a steady Vin of about 18 V, also in the working run, so it is not the cause. Worth a separate look at VDET/UVLO sensing.

## Ruled out (no change in the ~5 V stall)

- Soft-start slowed to about 69 ms (C26 replaced by 470 nF + 220 nF): inrush is not the cause.
- R22 1 mohm vs 10 mohm.
- Extra 1-2.2 nF across CSA/CSB (about 51 ns time constant with 2 x 10 ohm).
- Twisted Kelvin wires from R22 pads to the R26/R27 U6-side pads.
- CSA/CSB polarity: netlist confirms CSA (pin 22) is on R22 pad 3 (SW_1 side) and CSB (pin 21) on pad 2 (inductor side).

## Partial results

- 470 nF across CSA/CSB: Vout 7.6 V, PSU 536 mA, noisy switching (heavy filtering degrades the current loop).
- A 2.2 ohm resistor across CSA/CSB and a 4.7 ohm resistor each allowed the same successful behavior; the exact resistor fitted during the loaded test was not recorded.
- With a 10 ohm output load, the reported result was 12.6 V at 1.24 A over most of the input range. Exact input points and STATUS_BYTE readings for this loaded test were not recorded. This demonstrates about 15.6 W output, not the full 200 W design rating.

## Output measurement

- The LM51772 I2C registers used by the bring-up firmware provide the programmed VOUT target, not a live measured VOUT telemetry value. The firmware's `VOUT_mV` test log field is the target-register value.
- A separate output measurement is needed for firmware feedback/logging: an MCU ADC divider for voltage-only measurement, or a suitable monitor such as INA219 if output current/power telemetry is also wanted. Select the current-sense shunt for the intended maximum output current and dissipation.

## Feedback-mode bench rework

- The manufactured Rev 1 board uses external feedback through R3/R25. The successful I2C target tests used a bench rework: R3 was lifted and U6 FB pin 14 was wired to VCC2 pin 29 before startup, selecting internal feedback; D8 was set to DIV20 (`0x84`).
- The bench setup also changed R18/R17 from 76.8 kΩ/5.62 kΩ to 100 kΩ/5.1 kΩ for the VIN-FB divider. R61 remained 20 kΩ in the EN/UVLO divider; no R61 bench-value change was reported.
- This rework is not present in the Rev 1 PCB/BOM. Rev-next must explicitly choose and document a feedback configuration compatible with its firmware. If using I2C-controlled internal feedback, provide the FB-to-VCC2 connection and DIV20 configuration in the design, and verify measured Vout against the target. Do not treat the bench jumper as an existing PCB feature.

## ILIMCOMP/ISET bench rework

- Bench configuration reported 2026-11-07: R68 is removed, with a jumper from R32 pad 1 (`U6_VCC2`) to R68 pad 2 (`U6-ILIMCOMP_ISET`, U6 pin 16). R68 pad 1 is the connection to R46 (82 kΩ) toward `PWR_NEG`; with R68 removed, this jumper does not preserve the designed R68/R46 resistor path.
- Earlier bench notes report this temporary pin-16-to-VCC2 test did not change the approximately 3.56 V output stall. The exact current-limit behavior of the present wiring is not validated; do not treat it as a safe current-limit configuration or as evidence that the ISET/current-limit path is unneeded.
- This as-reported physical state supersedes the 2026-11-05 handoff's assumption that the R68/R46 path had been restored. Confirm the fitted wiring before further powered testing.

## Gate-resistor bench rework

- Bench configuration reported 2026-11-07: R69/R70/R71/R72 were changed from the Rev 1 10 Ω values to 22 Ω each. They are the series gate resistors for HO1/M1, LO1/M2, HO2/M4 and LO2/M3, respectively.
- The effect on gate timing, switching-node ringing, converter behavior and temperature has not yet been recorded. Validate those effects before treating 22 Ω as a Rev-next value.

## COMP capacitor value

- The Rev 1 PCB and BOM specify C24 = 22 nF; an earlier 220 nF entry in the derating review was a documentation error corrected in commit `31267e7`. Bench notes also report 22 nF, but the currently fitted component has not been physically confirmed.
- Carry 22 nF as the Rev-next design value, pending confirmation of the fitted Rev 1 component and the loop-compensation validation tracked in the C24 issue and converter qualification issue.

## Firmware

- `U6 BOOST ON|OFF` bypasses only the `Vin >= target + 3 V` guard in `u6_set_and_start`. Other guards and abort limits are unchanged.
- Datasheet: reg 0xD9 bit5 SEL_ISET_PIN resets to 1, so ILIM_THRESHOLD (0x0A) writes are ignored; the average current limit comes from the pin 16 resistors.

## Cautions and next steps

- With CSA/CSB shorted there is no peak-current protection. With a resistor across the sense inputs, the signal is attenuated and the effective peak-current threshold is raised; 2.2 ohm and 4.7 ohm across the two 10 ohm series resistors attenuate the differential signal to about 10% and 19%, respectively. Neither configuration is established as safe for full-power operation.
- Do not infer the full 200 W rating from the reported 10 ohm load test. Establish the final bench configuration, log VIN/VOUT/current/status and monitor component temperature before increasing power.
- Rev 2: decide between keeping LM51772 and switching to a dedicated charger such as BQ25756. If keeping U6, explicitly implement its chosen feedback mode, review CSA/CSB filtering/routing/C_DIFF per datasheet 7.3.10, and provide actual output-voltage measurement.
- Next work requested: inventory the known Rev 2 hardware, firmware and documentation changes, then create actionable GitHub issues for the selected work.
