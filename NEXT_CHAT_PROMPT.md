Current blocker: U6 LM51772-Q1 remains asserted at `FLT` and does not acknowledge I2C. R33 was replaced with zero ohms, and the separate Rev 0 `PGND` and `PWR_NEG` nets were joined using R31 pad 2 to R32 pad 2. With U6 powered, pin 9 measured approximately 0.0001 V and VCC2 measured 4.97 V. U6 pins 5/6 passed static continuity/isolation checks and were reworked. Marking and pin-1 orientation match the intended `LM51772QRHARQ1` in the RHA-40 footprint.

The flashed firmware now supports `i2cscan full`, which performs address-only probes over every legal 7-bit address `0x08` through `0x77`. A valid powered scan found ACKs only at AHT20 `0x38` and SSD1306 `0x3C`; both U6 addresses `0x6A` and `0x6B` NACKed, and no alternate address appeared. No register was accessed and no fault was cleared. Public GitHub LM51772 Python and C drivers confirm `0x6A`/`0x6B` and ordinary address-level readiness probing; no special wake-up or initialization sequence was found.

The logic analyzer proved that raw address byte `0xD4` reaches original U6 pins 5/6 with nine clean clocks and SDA remains high for NACK. U6 was then replaced, but the replacement also NACKed an address-only `0x6A` probe while `VCC2=4.95 V` and pin 9 was 0.009 V. A second analyzer capture directly at replacement-U6 pins 5/6 independently confirmed SDA bits `11010100` (`0xD4`) and SDA high on the ninth clock for NACK. PV is off. Do not repeat scans or replace U6 again. The next discriminating work is board-level: verify powered RST pin 38 and ground/reference integrity at AGND pin 17, PGND pin 28, and the exposed pad, then reassess configuration latching. Do not write registers or access `CLEAR_FAULTS` at `0x03`.

Hard limits remain: 6.49 V maximum PV, 20 mA current limit, M1-M4 absent, no battery/output source/load/panel/inverter/AC, and no switching. F1, U2, and U5 protection functions are bypassed. Keep `PA8`, `PB5`, and legacy `PA5` low; never drive `PB11`.
